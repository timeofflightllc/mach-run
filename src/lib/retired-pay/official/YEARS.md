Official DFAS monthly basic pay. Allowances, sea pay, and drill pay are not in these tables.

A retirement month uses the scale in effect that month. A blank longevity cell means the pay did not change, so the earlier cell still applies. Step 0 is "2 or less".

Where the scales come from
- June 1958 through January 2022, except the years already stored as their own files, from Internet Archive snapshots of the official DFAS PDFs linked on the military pay-chart index. Nothing was retyped from a second site.
- 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 stay in their own files. An uploaded year replaces the seed for that whole year.

What a 1960 entry uses
- The June 1, 1958 scale, until October 1, 1963. There is no separate 1959, 1960, 1961, or 1962 chart.

Months that are not January
- October 1963, September 1964, September 1965, July 1966, October 1967, July 1968, July 1969.
- January and October 1972. October 1972 is the chart dated 1 January 1973 and retroactive to 1 October 1972.
- October of each year 1974 through 1982.
- July 2000 and July 2001. Targeted raises. Officer over-20 basic pay did not move in July 2001; enlisted did.
- April 2007. The enlisted basic-pay raise. Officer basic pay is the January rate.

Scales that were not stored
- 1983 and 1986. Pay was frozen. The previous scale stays in effect.
- October 1971. Every DFAS snapshot of that file is the January 1972 chart.
- October 1973. Every DFAS snapshot of that file is the October 1972 retro chart, already stored.
- 2019 and later are the yearly files, not this historical set.
